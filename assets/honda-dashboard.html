HONDA BUSINESS MANAGEMENT DASHBOARD

A desktop app that puts a car business's rentals, repairs and sales in one place: log in, search 1,500 records in real time, add new entries that save straight to a local Excel workbook, and open an analytics view with KPIs, charts and one-click CSV exports.

PythonTkinterPandasMatplotlibopenpyxlExcel
65-SECOND WALKTHROUGH // LOGIN → RECORDS → LIVE SEARCH → ADD A RECORD → ANALYTICS

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Problem: a car business tracked rentals, repairs and sales by hand in separate spreadsheets, so simple questions like "which model sells the most?" took manual digging.

What I built: a Tkinter desktop application with a login screen and four workspaces: Rentals, Repairs, Sales and Analytics. Every record tab supports real-time search across every field, and new records typed into the app are validated and saved directly to a local Excel workbook through openpyxl, so the data survives restarts.

Analytics: the analytics view runs on pandas. It shows KPI cards (record counts and totals per business line, average repair cost), Matplotlib charts for monthly sales value, monthly service amounts, sales value by car model and the transaction mix, plus report tables for sales by model, top customers and repair-cost breakdown. A year filter recalculates everything, and one button exports all five analysis tables as CSVs.

The app ships with a generated sample dataset of 1,500 records (600 rentals, 600 repairs, 300 sales) so every view and chart works out of the box.

KEY FEATURES

1,500 RECORDS, ONE APP

Rentals, repairs and sales managed in separate tabs of a single window, loaded from local Excel workbooks.

REAL-TIME SEARCH

Typing in the search box filters every field of every record tab instantly, with a live match count.

LOCAL EXCEL PERSISTENCE

New records are validated and appended to the Excel workbook on save, so data persists between sessions with no database server.

PANDAS ANALYTICS

KPI cards, monthly trend charts, sales by model, top customers and repair breakdown, all recalculated by a year filter.

CSV EXPORT

One click writes all five analysis tables to CSV files for sharing or further work in Excel.

LOGIN SCREEN

A simple credential gate keeps the business data behind a login before the dashboard opens.
